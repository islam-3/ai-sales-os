## Read by hand

Every one of the 36 price replies asks for all three parts of the step:
a photo attached here in the chat, a name, and a WhatsApp number (most
with the country code). An automated pattern check flagged two replies
as having no name. Both were the pattern's own gaps: 名字 in Chinese, and
"isminizi" in Turkish. Neither model stated a price that the business's
material does not contain (0 of 36).

The code fallback (ensurePriceStep appending the fixed sentence) never
fired: the state-block line was enough for both models in every case.
The free test exercises it on the real dead-end replies from
2026-11-08. The "details" variant, for a business that does not need to
see the case, was not exercised live. test-clinic is a business that
does, and only test tenants may run this.

Measured cost: $0.9022 for the 36 conversations ($0.0312 per Sonnet
conversation, $0.0189 per Terra conversation). The $1.43 projection came
from one conversation measured on a cold cache.
